What each section is for

  • Today's visit becomes the S (subjective) part of your first SOAP note, in the client's own words.
  • Health history is where contraindications show up. Blood clots, recent surgery, pregnancy and varicose veins change where and how deep you can work, so check them before every first session and ask about changes at each visit.
  • Medications matter more than clients expect: blood thinners raise bruising risk and pain relievers can mask feedback on pressure.
  • Preferences save the first five minutes of every session.
  • Consent wording varies by state and insurer. Have your own reviewed; this one is a starting point, not legal advice.

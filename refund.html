Refund Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

30-day money-back guarantee

If Tablenote isn't right for your practice, ask for a refund within 30 days of your payment and you'll get the full amount back. No questions asked.

How to ask

  • Reply to your receipt email, or use the “Manage subscription” link in it.
  • Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, which issues the refund to your original payment method. Banks usually show it within 5 to 10 business days.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel any time from the link in your receipt email. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and it won't renew.
  • After 30 days, payments for the current period are not refunded, except where the law requires it.

Your notes after you cancel

Cancelling never locks your records. Notes stay on your device, and you can keep viewing them and exporting them as PDF, CSV or a full backup file. Only adding new clients beyond the free plan stops.

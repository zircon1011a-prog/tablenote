    Questions

    Before you try it

    Is Tablenote HIPAA compliant?

    During the beta your notes never leave your device, so we never receive or store client health information. The sync version will run on HIPAA-eligible infrastructure, and we'll sign a Business Associate Agreement with you before you use it for client records.

    Does it work on my phone?

    Yes. It runs in Safari or Chrome on any recent phone, tablet or laptop. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app.

    Can I bring notes from my current software?

    Not yet. Tell us what you use in the early-access form and we'll build the importers people ask for most.

    What happens to my notes if I stop paying?

    You keep export access. Download every note as PDF, CSV, or a full backup file at any time, including after you cancel.

    Does it handle booking or insurance claims?

    No. Tablenote is only for charting. Keep your booking and payment tools, and use Tablenote for the notes.

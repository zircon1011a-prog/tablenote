The left/right rule

Always chart the client's left and right, not yours. On the anterior (front) view the client faces you, so their left side is on your right. On the posterior (back) view their left is on your left. Mixing these up is the most common body chart mistake, and it matters when another therapist reads your notes.

A simple marking legend

  • Filled circle: pain or tenderness the client reports.
  • X: tension, hypertonicity or a trigger point you found.
  • Circle with a slash: an area to avoid or treat lightly, such as varicose veins, a recent injury or a skin condition.

Use the same symbols every session. Comparing this week's chart with last week's is the quickest way to show progress, to the client and in your records.

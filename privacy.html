Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026 · Beta

The short version

Your client notes never reach us. The beta app stores everything in your browser on your own device. The only information we receive is what you type into the early-access form.

Client notes in the app

  • Clients, SOAP notes, body chart marks and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use.
  • They are not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see, recover or delete them for you.
  • Clearing your browser data removes them. Use “Export → Full backup” to keep a copy.
  • The app page has no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

The early-access form

  • We collect your email address and your optional answers (which tool you use now, whether you want booking and payments, and the plan you picked).
  • We use them only to email you about Tablenote: when sync is ready and, if you reserved a price, before any billing starts. No newsletter, and we never sell or share your email for marketing.
  • The form is delivered to us by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service.
  • You can ask us to delete your email at any time by replying to any email we send you.

Visit counts on this website

The landing page (not the app) counts page visits and button clicks with hits.sh. It sets no cookies and we do not receive personal information from it.

Health information and HIPAA

During the beta we do not receive, store or transmit client health information, so your notes stay under your control on your device. Before any version of Tablenote stores notes on our servers, we will publish an updated policy and offer a Business Associate Agreement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. If a change affects how we use your email, we will tell you by email first.

Tablenote is an independent beta product.
